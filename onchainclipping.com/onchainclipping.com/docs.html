How Clipd works

Open marketplace for memecoin clipping. Funded in SOL. Paid in SOL.

For creators

Launch a campaign, set every term yourself, and fund the vault with at least $10 in SOL at the live quote. When the SOL lands, the campaign goes live on the public board. Clippers submit links. When views verify, the vault pays them.

You pick budget, pay per 1K, UGC rate, viral bonus, platforms, min views, and duration. Clipd does not set a rate card.

Launch a campaign

For clippers

Open Campaigns. Pick a live vault. Post on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or X. Submit the link. If the clip hits the campaign’s min views, you get paid from that vault in SOL — no withdrawal step.

See live campaigns

The vault

Each campaign gets its own Solana address. The USD budget is quoted into SOL and refreshed until the first deposit lands. After that the quote is frozen. A tiny shortfall from wallet rounding still counts as funded.

Payouts

Budget is shown in USD. Settlement is SOL. Views are checked on the link you submitted. When they verify, SOL leaves the campaign vault and goes to the clipper wallet.
